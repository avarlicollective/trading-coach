Daily Mindset Reminder
WAIT FOR THE SETUP. TRIM. TRAIL. STOP TRADING.

🎯 The Daily Goal

Enter each session with one objective: find a high conviction setup at a key level, execute your plan, take profit, and stop trading.

  • Identify the key level beforehand
  • Wait for the exact setup trigger
  • Trim majority at T1, lock in green
  • Trail runners, then walk away

🚫 What Destroys Accounts

  • Random trades — no setup, no entry
  • Trading in chop zones
  • Revenge trading after a loss
  • Full size after a win (greed)
  • Chasing breakouts / breakdown shorts
  • Not trimming when green
  • Fighting a strong trend
  • Ignoring the 7-pt red rule

⚡ The 7-Point Rule

If you are up 7 points and have NOT trimmed at T1, exit immediately. No trade goes red after 7 pts. This is non-negotiable.

🧘 Pre-Trade Mental Check

  • Am I at a key area or the chop zone?
  • Have I waited for acceptance/trigger?
  • Is my stop placed correctly?
  • Do I have clear T1 and T2 targets?
  • Have I already profited today?
  • Is this FOMO or a real setup?
  • Is the trend with me or against me?
